# NET-004: Review
Status: RESOLVED

Reviewed `origin/main...HEAD`: 8 source files, 6 test files, one deletion, plus docs.

## Findings

### 1. `HTTP status` and `Redirects followed` meant two different requests — fixed as `TASK-FIX-1`

The brand-asset rows were appended to the panel's `Origin` section, and two of them reused labels
that already appear under `Transport`, where they mean the *manifest's*. Two identically labelled
numbers about two different requests, in one scrolling panel, is a way to misdiagnose rather than a
way to diagnose — in the tool built to end exactly that.

Fixed before the first hosted run: its own `Brand asset` heading, and the existing kind row moved
into it as `Slot shows` so no label repeats its heading.

### 2. The planned negative control for `publishesBrandAsset` proves nothing — recorded, not fixed

`TASK-3` planned to relax `===` to `==` and watch the superseded case fail. It does not fail:
`SiteSkinConfiguration` is a plain class with no `equals`, so the two are the same comparison today.
The control that works is deleting the guard, which was run.

Kept `===` and wrote both facts into the KDoc and the test. The day that type becomes a `data class`
is the day the difference starts mattering, and nothing else would notice.

### 3. Two origin spellings have to agree, and nothing asserted it — verified, no change

The sink keys on `configuration.origin` (core's `TrustedOrigin.value`) while the inspector looks up
`SiteOrigin.canonical`. A silent disagreement would make the panel show `Still loading` forever and
the harness time out.

They agree by construction — `OriginPolicy.parse` builds `"https://$authority"` with the default port
elided, which is `SiteOrigin.canonical`'s exact rule — and empirically: run 16 found the record under
`https://denrzv.github.io`. No code change; noted here because "verified" and "never checked" look
identical afterwards.

### 4. Claims the evidence does not support — cut

Two drafts overstated what the hosted runs proved:

- The retry has `attempts=1` in every hosted run, because neither run's network blinked. It is
  justified by run 16's *recorded failure* and pinned by unit tests, not by a hosted run that never
  exercised it. `reports/qa/NET-004.md` says so, and the field is in the artifact so nobody has to
  take it on trust.
- Runs 16 and 17's contested captures are `CI-005`'s guard working, not a regression this ticket
  introduced or a reason to weaken it. The added wait was under a second in both. `CI-006` carries it.

## Deliberate deviations from the plan

- `BrandAssetStage` / `BrandAssetTrace` live in `SiteSkinTrace.kt` and the sink in
  `SiteSkinTraceSink.kt`, following `TraceTransportOutcome`'s placement. The trace vocabulary is the
  inspector's; the refusal vocabulary is the transport's, beside `FetchRejection`.
- `BrandAssetRejection` gained a ninth case the plan did not list: a redirect carrying no `Location`
  is `MALFORMED_URL` rather than being folded into the cross-origin refusal. Different things to fix.
- The plan's `SiteSkinTraceNeutralityTest` extension was dropped. Inventing a sink for the loader in
  order to prove it neutral would have created the divergence the test exists to detect.

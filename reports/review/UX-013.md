Date: 2026-08-15
Status: RESOLVED

Reviewed `277fe6d..4829c78`: the complete ticket artifacts, closed presentation projection, curved

## Summary

The trust boundary is correctly narrower than the existing validated SiteSkin model: only the
already-guarded colour scheme crosses into expressive presentation, while theme/motion selection is
browser-owned and geometry is compiled. One medium architecture finding remains: the primitive
named and documented as the floating dock does not itself create the floating inset. It paints a
rounded full-width bar and delegates the defining geometry to a future caller, contrary to this
ticket's purpose of owning that geometry once.

## Findings

### FINDING-1 · Medium · the floating dock does not own its floating inset

**File:** `app/src/main/java/app/webora/browser/siteskin/ExpressiveSiteSkinChrome.kt`

Current code applies `fillMaxWidth()` directly to the painted, clipped `Row`. At 320 dp its surface
touches both container edges. A future UX-015 caller must add outer horizontal padding to produce
the M9 floating geometry, which makes the defining dimension caller policy rather than the compiled
primitive policy this ticket exists to establish. The row also leaves its fixed-height controls
top-aligned rather than centering them in the 60 dp surface.

Fix as `TASK-FIX-1`: add a full-width outer `Box` with a compiled horizontal inset, keep the tagged
painted `Row` inside it, and center content vertically. Extend the instrumentation contract to prove
the dock's visible/tagged bounds are inset from a 320 dp parent without the caller supplying padding.

## Not findings

- **The motion policy is not consumed by an animation yet.** There is no state transition in these
  unused containers to animate. Inventing one would create motion solely to exercise the enum;
  UX-014/UX-015 must use the closed standard/reduced choice when they introduce real transitions.
- **The JVM test does not trust `Path.getBounds()`.** Android's Compose `Path` is a stub under local
  JVM tests and returns an empty rectangle. The test checks the actual `Outline.Generic` plus the
  pure coordinates fed into it, while device instrumentation owns real clip/layout evidence.
- **`controlY` lies below the layout height.** A quadratic control point is not necessarily on the
  curve; this is what produces the soft center drop while the resulting curve stays bounded by the
  two shoulders and center extremum.
- **The header accepts a generic content slot.** That does not make it remote layout. The primitive
  has no configuration/raw DTO access; UX-014 supplies browser-authored identity/control composition
  and trusted bounded brand content at a local call site.
- **Global safe-drawing insets are absent.** `BrowserScreen` already applies them once. Reading them
  again here would double-pad both future surfaces rather than make them safer.
- **No screenshot exists.** The primitives are intentionally unwired and cause no perceptible
  runnable-app change; photographing current chrome would provide no evidence about this ticket.

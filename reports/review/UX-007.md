## Not findings

- A vertical stack is used even when a wider row could fit. This is intentional adaptive behavior:
  one deterministic hierarchy avoids translation- and font-dependent split rows.
- The complete block is supplied through `confirmButton` with no `dismissButton`. Material's slot
  names do not determine behavior; consolidating them prevents two unrelated action groups.
- Never is a text button rather than a destructive filled button. It is persistent but reversible
  in Settings; lower emphasis distinguishes permanence without hiding the choice.
- Pixel evidence is absent. This checkout has neither KVM nor a device, so policy requires compiled
  instrumentation rather than a software-only emulator.

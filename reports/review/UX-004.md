## Not findings

- **A passive `Switch` nested in a toggleable row is intentional.** The row is the single action and supplies the browser-authored name/state; the switch is the visual value indicator. Giving both callbacks would create two adjacent semantic actions for one setting.
- **Home still shows empty recents and favourites.** UX-004 is a surface refresh, not history/favourites persistence; honest empty states are the roadmap scope.
- **The full origin is not the reset button's visible label.** It is wrapping primary row text and remains in the compact reset action's accessible description, satisfying both visual identity and control-navigation requirements.
- **Direct Material `Surface`, `Card`, `Switch`, and `OutlinedTextField` are not palette escapes.** They read the complete Material role mapping supplied by `WeboraTheme`; raw browser button imports remain convention-gated.

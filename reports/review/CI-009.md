## Findings

### FINDING-1 · Medium · hosted evidence state readiness
**File:** `app/src/androidTest/java/app/webora/browser/visual/LiveSiteScreenshotTest.kt`

After clicking the remote product link, the test immediately re-observes persistent integrated tags
and captures. Those tags and already-rendered storefront pixels can remain valid while WebView is
still navigating, so `04-happy-days-product.png` can photograph the storefront. The inverse races
exist after Back and Forward. A later enabled-control assertion may fail the job, but an `always()`
screenshot upload can still expose a misleading partial frame, and Back can already be enabled from
the native-Home history before product navigation.

Fix: use the existing black-box accessible product link as a transition sentinel: wait until its
clickable node disappears before product/forward captures and returns before the Back storefront
capture. This does not establish trust; browser tags and `ScreenEvidenceGuard` continue to do that.
Pin those readiness markers in the JVM source contract.

## Not findings

- **No production frame registry.** Filenames are an instrumentation/JVM evidence contract, not app
  state. Moving them into production would widen rather than improve the architecture.
- **The hub frame still requires page pixels.** The modal is browser-owned, but its evidence includes
  the rendered Bloom context behind it. Reusing the existing candidate verdict is stricter than
  exempting the frame.
- **Home and consent skip the rendered-page requirement.** Home has no WebView rectangle; consent
  proves browser-owned attribution over a deliberately dimmed page. Both still require app window
  ownership and the exact returned bitmap.
- **Eight-column preview width is not production hard-coding.** The composer remains dynamic; the
  fixture pins the current story and the workflow compares actual PNG and tile counts.
- **Renaming old frames is intentional.** Zero-padded chronological filenames are the caption and
  sort contract; preserving `04-regular` would place teardown before the added story.

## Not findings

- The Back control sits inside the SiteSkin top-bar geometry, but its surface does not use the passed
  SiteSkin scheme. `MaterialTheme.colorScheme.surfaceContainer`, the drawable, label, state, and
  callback are all browser-owned.
- Forward and reload are not included. The ticket closes the observed inaccessible Back gap without
  turning the site's bottom navigation into a duplicate browser dock.
- Back remains visible when disabled. This matches regular browser chrome, avoids history-driven
  layout shifts, and communicates that the unavailable action is browser navigation.
- The top bar still uses manifest colours for its branded outer surface. That is the existing trusted
  SiteSkin contract; only the new browser control requires a visibly independent container.

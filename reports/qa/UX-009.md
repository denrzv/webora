## Edge cases

- **invalid manifest → regular browser mode** — N/A, no logic change. Nothing in this ticket runs on
  a validation path; a rejected manifest still ends in regular browsing, and regular chrome's shapes
  come from `BrowserChrome`'s directly named pill, which did not move.
- **origin change / redirect** — N/A, no logic change. `SKIN-004`'s origin/generation recheck is
  untouched; a shape token is not consulted by it.
- **offline with cached manifest** — N/A, no logic change. `NET-002`'s cache paths are untouched.
- **oversized or malformed payload** — N/A, no logic change. Core's parse bounds are untouched.
- **accessibility (TalkBack, font scale)** — **not N/A, and it is the interesting one.** The defect
  was invisible to TalkBack and visible only in pixels: the semantics tree carried the complete
  heading throughout, which is why every instrumented assertion stayed green across two hosted runs.
  So this change improves the *visual* half of a guarantee whose spoken half was never broken, and
  it improves it under font scaling too — a 28 dp corner cannot eat a line that grows, where a
  half-width corner ate more of every line the taller the dialog got. `A11Y-001`'s rule that a visual
  bound needs a semantics counterpart holds here in reverse, and is worth recording in that
  direction: a semantics guarantee needs a *visual* counterpart, and nothing in the JVM gate can
  supply one.

## Result
Status: QA_PASSED
Notes:

**Passed on the code, with criterion 7 open and named.** Scenarios 1–10 are green and the two that
matter carry a negative control. What the JVM gate proves is complete for the *cause*: the container
role no longer resolves to a stadium at any width a dialog can reach, and the assertion that says so
fails when the pill is put back. What it cannot prove is the *effect* — that a user now sees an
intact heading — because nothing in this repository can fail on a clipped surface. That is the hosted
frame's job, and this run did not deliver one.

**Scenario 2 is the control that matters, and its value is the number rather than the colour.** A
red-before/green-after pair only says the test is sensitive to *something*. `140.0px on a 280.0px
box` says it is sensitive to the specific arithmetic in the defect's story: `CornerBasedShape`
scaling adjacent corner pairs by `minDimension / sum`. Had the control printed 999, the clamp would
have been a claim about Compose rather than an observation of it, and the ceiling would have been
guarding the wrong thing.

**Scenario 4 is the finding worth carrying forward.** `every shape corner comes from the compiled
radii` was green before this ticket, green during the defect, and green under the negative control.
It asks where a value came from; nothing asked whether the value fits the box that reads it. Any
future token scale wants both questions, and this is the cheap example of what the second one buys.

**The unmet criterion 5 is recorded in the tasklist rather than here as a pass.** The instrumented
case was found during `/review` to duplicate an assertion the file already made, and it was cut. The
requirement it was written for was already covered; what was genuinely missing — the record that the
covering assertion cannot see a clip — is now attached to that assertion.

never satisfied. Its own tasklist required two consecutive cold hosted runs with:

```text

```

No such two-run acceptance was recorded in this report or the tasklist. The last material CI-009

## Why it is superseded

The product contract subsequently changed through UX-021, UX-022 and BROWSE-011:

- browser-owned trust/security semantics changed;
- SiteSkin action presentation became `auto | bouquet | drawer`, with `auto -> drawer`;
- current browser navigation/mount semantics moved beyond the original M9 acceptance story.

Issue #115 / CI-010 is now the authoritative hosted acceptance for the current runtime contract and
the only deployed fixture in scope, `https://denrzv.github.io/apps/bloom/`.

CI-009 issue #88 is closed as superseded/not-planned rather than completed acceptance. This preserves
the useful historical evidence without claiming that its obsolete two-run gate passed.

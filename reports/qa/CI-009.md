## Hosted failure #33

- Run: `31929274541`, commit `b996db362f5c4648c13a32888a982b17d6299b9e`.
- Emulator readiness: PASS — settled after 30 samples / 94 seconds with final quiet samples 7%, 3%, 3%.
- Build: PASS.
- Captures before failure: `01-home.png`, `02-siteskin-consent.png`, `03-bloom-storefront.png`.
- Failure: `Bloom did not expose the Happy Days Bouquet product link` in
  `exerciseExpressiveBloomJourney()`.
- Root cause: the live storefront's visible clickable title is `Happy Days`; `Happy Days Bouquet` is
  product-page text and part of the image link's accessible label. The Popular Picks rail also sits
  below the initial storefront viewport. The failed selector therefore could not be satisfied by
  merely waiting longer.
- Resolution: TASK-FIX-2 uses `By.text("Happy Days").clickable(true)` as the black-box interaction and
  readiness seam and performs bounded user-equivalent vertical swipes until that node is visibly
  exposed. This changes no production browser behavior and does not make remote text a trust signal.

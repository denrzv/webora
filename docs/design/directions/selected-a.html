Why frame 03 is cheap here and expensive in B

A's identity chip sits beside the address field in both states, so switching to edit mode is an ordinary focus change on an ordinary text field. There is no second component and no state machine — C1 is satisfied by the layout rather than by behaviour.

That is the whole difference in effort between A and B, and it is the reason A's risk was recorded as low: nothing here fights an existing gate.

The honest trade, seen rather than argued

A is materially cheaper to build: C1 falls out of the layout, the dock is thumb-reachable, and it fights none of the existing gates. That is a real advantage and it is why its risk was recorded as low.

What frames 04–05 cost is chrome height — pill plus chip plus dock, against B's 48 dp bar — and softness of the boundary. B's amendment made the identity strip a colour the site can never take; A's chrome is tonal and rounded like the site's own bar. The security mechanism is intact either way, because the chip is browser-owned text from securityPresentation. The question is whether it reads as browser at a glance.

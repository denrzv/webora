# CI-008: Research
Status: RESEARCH_READY

## Existing evidence path

- `LiveSiteScreenshotTest.capturesLiveBloomFlowersSiteSkinJourney` captures Home, consent, and the
  integrated Bloom frame, then calls `captureRegularBrowsingEvidence`.
- That helper finds `SITESKIN_BACK_TAG`, clicks it, waits for the native Home address field, submits
  `example.com`, requires regular browser chrome and SiteSkin absence, and captures frame 04 through
  the unchanged ownership/rendered guard.
- Runs 30 and 31 stopped at the address-field wait because the tagged Back control was disabled.
  `BROWSE-008` now makes every non-Home tab Back-capable, with live WebView history taking priority
  over the native Home fallback.
- Compose testing distinguishes presence/display from enabled action semantics. `performClick()` by
  itself is a poor diagnostic for this prerequisite; `assertIsDisplayed().assertIsEnabled()` names
  the product contract at the transition boundary.

## Origins and trust boundary

- The accepted integrated state belongs to exact origin `https://denrzv.github.io`; the regular
  destination is `https://example.com` after browser resolution.
- Neither origin, its page, nor a SiteSkin manifest may decide whether browser Back exists or is
  enabled. `SITESKIN_BACK_TAG` identifies a browser-owned affordance inside integrated chrome.
- The manifest still cannot affect the assertion, click, address input, destination, frame names,
  capture verdict, or artifact presentation.
- Browser-controlled code must continue to own Home fallback, regular identity, SiteSkin teardown,
  ownership/rendered checks, and evidence artifact integrity.

## Risks and checks

- **False confidence from compilation:** compilation proves matcher/API correctness, not the live
  remote journey. QA must keep hosted execution environment-limited when no remote/device exists.
- **Harness workaround:** no direct controller call, seeded Home state, sleep, retry, or longer wait
  is permitted.
- **Security regression:** all post-transition regular-presence and SiteSkin-absence checks remain.
- **Weak assertion:** a temporary `assertIsNotEnabled()` mutation is the negative control; it must
  fail compilation because that matcher is intentionally absent or, when performed at runtime, fail
  against the fixed product. Static source checks additionally pin the enabled assertion before click.
